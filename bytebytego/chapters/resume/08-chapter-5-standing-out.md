# 08: Standing Out

## Core Idea
Stand out by describing the results, impact, and your specific contribution with numbers, active language, and job-tailored keywords — not generic responsibilities.

## Frameworks & Principles
- **Results, impact, contribution**: "Accomplished {impact} as measured by {number} by doing {specific contribution}." Use numbers, active verbs ("led", "drove", "improved", "rolled out"), and name technologies near the end.
- **Master resume → tailored versions**: keep a long master resume, then trim and mirror the job description's language for each application.
- **Don't be humble**: talk about yourself (not "we"), show side projects and open-source contributions, mention your learnings, and drop negatives (failed projects, low GPA).
- **Keyword stuffing done sensibly**: include relevant keywords in a short Technologies/Skills section and sprinkled in experience — human-readable, not a dump.

## Key Concepts
- **Generalist vs. specific-technology companies**: big tech wants breadth, impact metrics, and CS fundamentals; smaller/non-tech companies screen for exact technologies, years of experience, and certifications.
- **Impact metrics**: RPS, test coverage %, infra cost savings, users, customer teams, latency reductions, revenue.
- **Responsibilities vs. accomplishments**: most people list responsibilities; accomplishments (results) are what get you hired.

## Anti-patterns
- **Generic descriptions** ("built a tool used widely") with no numbers or specifics — indistinguishable from every other applicant.
- **"We"/"team" language and passive phrasing** — undersells your contribution; use first person.
- **Cramming keywords** into unreadable sentences; overdoing it reads unprofessional and lands you in "maybe."

## Key Takeaways
1. Aim for at least one number per bullet supporting the story of your impact.
2. Create a tailored resume version per job description, mirroring its keywords and active verbs.
3. Differentiate generalist vs. specific-technology resumes and customize each accordingly.
