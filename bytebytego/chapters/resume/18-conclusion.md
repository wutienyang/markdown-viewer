# 18: Conclusion

## Core Idea
The resume screen is just the first step in a long hiring process; a resume that represents you fairly clears that first step at more companies.

## Frameworks & Principles
- **Full journey**: pipeline → resume basics → structure → mistakes → polish exercises → template principles → before/after examples.
- **Fairness as the bar**: aim to represent yourself fairly and maximize your pass rate at the screen.

## Key Concepts
- **Resume screen as step one**: the very first filter in a longer process; the whole book optimizes for clearing it.

## Anti-patterns
- **Over-investing in format** while neglecting content and tailoring.
- **Expecting one resume to work everywhere** without per-job customization.

## Key Takeaways
1. Apply the book's advice end to end: understand the pipeline, follow basics, structure by level, stand out, avoid mistakes, and polish.
2. Tailor each submission and keep iterating with feedback.
